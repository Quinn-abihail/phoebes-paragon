Run like it's your own kitchen and your own list.

Phoebe's Paragon started with a simple idea: the two chores people put off longest — cooking properly and shopping properly — shouldn't take up the hours you don't have. So we do them for you, the way you'd do them yourself if you had the time.

Every order is treated as a standing relationship, not a one-off task. We learn what you like, what you avoid, and how you like things done, so each booking gets easier than the last.

Two services. One standard of care.
